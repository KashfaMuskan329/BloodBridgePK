TRANSPARENT BY DESIGN

Real listings. Honest limitations.

Facility names and addresses come from public directories. Coordinates are approximate neighborhood anchors, not verified entrance locations. Some regulatory listings are historical, so current status must be checked. No blood inventory, patient records, availability or clinical matching is collected or claimed.

ABOUT BLOODBRIDGE PK

Care is more than a pin.

BloodBridge PK is a prototype and educational simulation focused on the journey of thalassaemia families in Lahore and Islamabad. It uses publicly listed facility information and clearly marked fictional scenarios.

What is realFacility names, publicly listed addresses and links, Amazon Location map/routing when configured.
What is simulatedPatient stories, selected starting points, camp placement and distance-impact comparisons.

Not a medical or emergency service. Do not rely on the map for blood supply, suitability, treatment or urgent decision-making. Call the relevant facility and healthcare professional directly.
